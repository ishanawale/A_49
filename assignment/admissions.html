Admissions

Admission information for aspiring engineering students.

Admission Information: Admissions are carried out through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Government of Maharashtra.

Admission Process

Admissions are carried out through the Centralized Admission Process (CAP) conducted by the Government of Maharashtra.

Eligibility

  • Passed HSC (Science).
  • Qualified MHT-CET or JEE Main.
  • Documents verified through CAP.

Required Documents

  • 10th Marksheet
  • 12th Marksheet
  • MHT-CET/JEE Scorecard
  • Leaving Certificate
  • Caste Certificate (if applicable)
  • Passport-size Photographs
